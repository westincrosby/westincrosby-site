Questions You Might Have

Why is there no sales call?
Because you don't need one. Everything you need to decide — price, what's included, how it works — is already on this page. If something's still unclear, DM me directly and we can schedule a call.
How does payment and commitment work?
It's a 12-week commitment, paid in full upfront. After your 12 weeks are up, you can continue week to week and cancel anytime — no long-term lock-in.
What if I fall behind or miss a week?
Although that's not recommended, life happens. We just pick up and keep adjusting from your next check-in — missing a week doesn't reset anything.
Do I need a gym membership?
No. Programming is built around whatever you actually have access to, whether that's a full gym or a couple of dumbbells at home.
What if the plan isn't working for me?
It's never static. Every week's check-in feeds back into the plan, so it's constantly getting adjusted off your real numbers, not left on autopilot for 12 weeks.
Do you work with beginners?
Yes. The plan is built around your actual experience level, whether this is your first real program or you've been training for years.
What if I have an injury or old pain?
Tell me in your application. It gets built around it from day one, not something you have to work around yourself.

FAQ

Questions You Might Have

What do I actually receive?
The full 8-week program for whichever modality you pick, as a PDF with all 40 sessions mapped out, plus an editable PDF tracker to log your reps, weights, and notes. Both land in your inbox right after purchase.
Can I do this at home?
Yes — each program is built around minimal equipment. Kettlebell needs one (or two) kettlebells, Dumbbell needs a pair of dumbbells, and Calisthenics needs zero equipment at all. Garage, backyard, hotel room, wherever you've got a little space.
How much time do I need?
Most sessions run 30–45 minutes, 5 days a week. Short on a given day? Trim the volume and still hit the intent of the session.
What level athlete is this built for?
Built for someone who's trained before and can move without hand-holding — not a first-ever program, and not elite-only either. Show up and follow the plan, and it progresses with you.
